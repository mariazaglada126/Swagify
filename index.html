    How it works

    From your idea to a figure on your shelf. Every piece is made by hand, and you see and approve each stage before we move on.

    Seven steps to your figure

    1. 01

      Build your order

      Pick the quantity and the main options in the calculator: size, details, painting, figures or keychains. You’ll see an estimated price right away.

      Open the calculator
    2. 02

      Let’s talk in DM

      We message you on Instagram and go through every idea in detail: pose, outfit, colors, accessories, mood.

    3. 03

      Send references

      The more we see, the closer the result. Send photos from a few angles and any style references you like.

      • A person
      • A character
      • A pet
      • An object
      • Style refs
    4. 04

      2D concept

      We draw a concept of your figure. You check it and ask for changes until it feels right.

    5. 05

      3D model

      The approved concept becomes a 3D model. We show it to you from every side before anything is made.

    6. 06

      Your figure + approval

      We make and paint the figure by hand, then send you photos or a video so you can approve it before shipping.

    7. 07

      Shipping

      We pack your figure carefully and ship it to you. Shipping isn’t free: its cost depends on where you are and is paid separately — we’ll tell you the exact price in DM.
